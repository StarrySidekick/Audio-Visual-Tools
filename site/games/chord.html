How chords are built

A triad is three notes stacked in 3rds: a root, a 3rd and a 5th. The quality (major, minor, diminished, augmented) comes from which kind of 3rds you stack. Add another 3rd on top and you get a 7th chord.

The numbers under each answer are its formula: scale degrees measured against the major scale of the root. "b3" means a 3rd lowered by a half step. The 3rd is the main clue for major versus minor, so listen to the middle note of the arpeggio.

Harmonize It

A four-bar melody. Pick a chord for each bar. Try your chords by ear, then check.

Settings

The one rule this game checks

The notes on the strong beats (beats 1 and 3 of each bar, or the held note in the last bar) should be chord tones: notes that belong to the chord underneath. Notes on the weak beats can be passing notes that step between chord tones. That is the basic logic of harmonizing a melody, and it is why several chords can be "right" for the same bar.

How to work it out: read the note on beat 1 and the note on beat 3, then find the chord that contains both. For example, C and E fit C major (C E G) and A minor (A C E). Then use your ear: "Hear with my chords" plays your choices under the melody. Ending on I gives the strongest sense of arrival.

The key signature at the start of the staff tells you which notes are sharp or flat for the whole piece. Keys: 1–6 pick a numeral, Backspace undoes, Space plays the melody.

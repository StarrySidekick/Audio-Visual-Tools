What an interval is

An interval is the distance between two notes, counted in half steps (one key to the very next key, black or white). Its name has a size (2nd, 3rd, 4th...) counted in letter names, and a quality (major, minor, perfect) that pins down the exact number of half steps.

Song openings are a classic memory hook: the first two notes of a tune you know by heart give you a reference for each interval. They show up in the feedback when you answer.

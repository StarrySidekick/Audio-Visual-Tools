What you are training

Relative pitch is hearing a note in relation to one you already know. With the C reference on, you are really measuring the distance (the interval) from C, which is how most musicians identify notes. Absolute pitch (perfect pitch) is naming a note with no reference. It is rare in adults, so treat the no-reference mode as a curiosity.

Keyboard shortcut: press the letter (C, D, E, F, G, A, B) to answer with a white key.

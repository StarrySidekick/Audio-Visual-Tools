Reading the staff

The five lines and four spaces each stand for one letter name. The clef tells you which: the treble clef curls around the G line (second from the bottom), and the bass clef's two dots surround the F line (second from the top). Ledger lines are short extra lines for notes above or below the staff. Middle C sits on one ledger line below treble or one above bass.

Mnemonics, bottom to top. Treble lines: Every Good Boy Does Fine. Treble spaces: F A C E. Bass lines: Good Boys Do Fine Always. Bass spaces: All Cows Eat Grass.

Keyboard shortcut: press the letter key to answer in letter mode. Sprint mode is where this one gets fun.

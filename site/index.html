On the workbench

  • Pitch PondFrogs croak notes. Sing or tap the matching pitch to hop them onto lily pads before the heron arrives.
  • Chord KitchenOrders come in ("one Dm7, extra 9th"). Drag note ingredients onto the staff plate and serve before the ticket expires.
  • Interval ElevatorAn elevator that only moves by the interval you hear. Get passengers to the right floor.
  • Mode WeatherEach mode is a weather system. Hear a melody, forecast the mode. Lydian is sunshine, Locrian is fog.
  • Staff InvadersNotes descend the staff. Type their letter to zap them. Speeds up as you go.
